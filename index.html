Clip Inbox

A personal publishing tool

Clip Inbox is a private tool an individual creator uses to send short, edited clips to their own TikTok account as drafts. Every clip is made from public-domain or openly licensed footage and credits its original creator and source. Each draft is reviewed and published by hand in the TikTok app.
