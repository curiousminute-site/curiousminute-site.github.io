Privacy Policy

Effective 3 November 2026

Clip Inbox is a personal tool used by one person to send videos they edited to their own TikTok account as drafts. It is not offered to the public, collects no data about anyone else, and shares nothing with anyone except TikTok.

1Who runs the app and who uses it

Clip Inbox is operated by an individual creator for their own TikTok account. The only TikTok accounts that can connect to it are accounts owned by that creator. There are no public sign-ups.

2What the app accesses

When the account owner logs in with TikTok, the app receives only what TikTok grants for these permissions:

  • user.info.basic: the account's open ID, display name and avatar, used only to confirm the right account is connected.
  • video.upload: permission to send video files to that account's TikTok inbox as drafts. The account owner reviews, edits and publishes every draft themselves in the TikTok app.

TikTok also issues access and refresh tokens so the app can keep uploading without logging in again. The app does not access followers, messages, comments, viewing history, contacts or any other account data.

3How the data is stored

Tokens and account details are stored only on the operator's own computer, in a file readable only by the operator's user account. They are never sent anywhere except to TikTok's own API when uploading or refreshing access.

4What the app does not do

  • No selling, renting or sharing of data with third parties.
  • No advertising, analytics, tracking or cookies. The app has no website or user interface for the public.
  • No processing of data about other TikTok users.

5Videos

The videos uploaded are edited clips of public-domain or openly licensed footage, and each one credits its original creator, licence and source. The app uploads only videos the operator has prepared and approved.

6Retention and removal

Tokens are kept until access is revoked. Access can be revoked at any time from the app permissions section of the TikTok account's settings, after which the stored tokens stop working and are deleted by the operator.

7Children

The app is not directed at children and is used only by its adult operator.

8Changes

If the app's use of data changes, this policy will be updated and the effective date above changed.

Questions about this policy can be sent to the operator through the TikTok account this app is connected to. TikTok's own handling of data is described in TikTok's privacy policy. Clip Inbox is not affiliated with or endorsed by TikTok.
